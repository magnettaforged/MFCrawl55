Dungeon Crawler v0.9.0 Monster Rework

- Added monster family action pools with rarity-gated abilities.
- Added weighted simulated AI with conditional healing, defending, poison, buffs, magic, and charm.
- All monsters can defend for 50% reduction unless a stronger family guard action is defined.
- Human/humanoid monsters can use limited healing items; undead never use potions.
- Life steal no longer heals against undead monsters.
- Added new monster families from the supplied WebP manifest.
- Mirrored image pairs remain pooled as visual variants.
- Added exact biome pools for a/b/c/d/f/g and matching boss columns.
- Crystal favors magic users; beach favors marine life/pirates; winter favors ice casters; forest favors goblins/orcs/mushrooms; pyramids mix castle/treasure hunters without marine life.
